AION2 Pet Tracker: installation
===============================

Needs: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), AION 2 Global (EU / NA), and Npcap.

1. Install Npcap (free capture driver): https://npcap.com/#download with its default options.
2. Run AION2PetTracker.exe. Nothing to install or unzip; it can live anywhere (Desktop, Downloads...).
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" -> "Run anyway": the program is not signed
   with a paid certificate. On PCs with Smart App Control turned on, Windows blocks unsigned programs outright.
3. Start the tracker BEFORE you log in to your character: the game sends your pet collection once, at login.
   After that the tracker remembers it, and starting it mid-session works too.

Using it
The small "Nearby pets" overlay lists the monsters around you that drop pet souls, with the pet's level and
souls toward the next level (5 to unlock, 25, 75). Pets whose mobs you just killed stay listed for 5 minutes.
  - In game, hold Alt to get the cursor. Hover the overlay: - / + change its size, x hides it. Drag to move it.
  - The pet you are farming sits on top; it follows your latest soul drop. Click a pet in the "more pets nearby"
    list to pin it instead; the pin icon lets go again.
  - Hover a row and click the x to remove that pet until its mobs show up again.
  - Ctrl+Shift+P shows / hides it (change it in Settings, from the tray icon's menu).

The gold "A" in the notification area (next to the clock) opens the menu. The tracker is built on a DPS meter,
which stays out of sight; Ctrl+Shift+D opens it, Settings -> "Show the DPS meter" brings it back for good.

Settings and pet progress live in %AppData%\AION2PetTracker. Uninstall: delete the exe (and that folder).

The tracker only listens to your own game's network traffic. It never reads game memory, injects anything or
sends anything to the game. Third-party tools may still be against the game's terms: use at your own risk.
